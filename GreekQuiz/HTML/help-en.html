GreekQuiz Help

Main Modes of Operation

On the main screen, you can choose one of four modes to study words:

  • I will write: You are shown a word in the language you are learning, and you must type its translation.
  • Quiz: You are shown a word and four possible answers. You need to choose the correct one.
  • Flashcards: Classic flashcards for memorization. Tap the card to see the translation. Swipe left or right to move to the next or previous word.
  • Talk show: A mode for listening to words. The app alternately pronounces the word in the language you are learning and its translation. This mode works with the screen locked and is controlled by headphones.

Managing Dictionaries

The app works with dictionaries that you can download and select for studying.

  • On first launch, the app automatically downloads the standard set of dictionaries.
  • To select which dictionaries to use for studying, tap the the vertical books icon in the top right corner.
  • In the window that opens, you can enable and disable dictionaries. Words from enabled dictionaries will be used in all modes.
  • To see the full list of words in the selected dictionaries, tap the "Show words" button.

Settings

Tap the gearicon to open the settings screen.

Main Settings

  • Show transcription: Enables or disables the display of transcription under Greek words.
  • Auto-pronounce sound: If enabled, the word in the learning language will be pronounced automatically when you move to it.
  • Answer sounds: If enabled, in "Quiz" mode, the answer option you tap on will be pronounced.

Quiz Settings

  • Use all words: When this option is enabled, in "Quiz" mode, words from all downloaded dictionaries can be used as incorrect answer choices. The question word itself is always taken only from your selected dictionaries.

Language Settings

  • Learning language: Choose which language the question words will be shown in.
  • Answer language: Choose which language you will provide the translation in (or in which language the answer will be shown).
  • Interface language: Choose the language for all menus and buttons in the app.

Dictionaries

  • You can choose the source for downloading dictionaries: standard or from your own link (URL).
  • Tapping the "Download and save dictionaries" button will completely clear the old dictionaries and download new ones.

Rules

Tap the book icon to read the detailed rules and a description of the learning methodology.
